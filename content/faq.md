# Frequently asked questions

## What does Dataceam actually do?

Dataceam is a referrer only. I introduce companies to AI-data buyers, and point experts to roles on AI-training platforms. I don't buy, store, clean or sell data, I don't hire anyone, and I'm not a party to any deal.

## Who are you?

I'm Syed Jawad, based in Malta. I run Dataceam on my own. I'm not employed by any buyer or platform, and I can't sign or promise anything on their behalf.

## What data counts?

Records your company created in the normal course of business, for example:

- CRM records and sales notes
- Support tickets and customer conversations
- Internal documents, wikis and SOPs
- Source code and commit history
- Project boards and task histories
- Invoices and operational records

Older, larger and well-organised sets tend to be most useful. Whether a specific set fits depends on what buyers need at the time.

## What about privacy and personal data?

I never receive your data. If you go ahead, you agree directly with the buyer what's in scope, how personal data is removed or de-identified, who does it, and what the buyer may use the data for. Get each of those in your contract, and take your own data-protection advice. You can say no at any stage before signing.

## Who pays, and what does it cost me?

Nothing. If a deal goes ahead, the buyer may pay me a referral fee. Any licence fee for your data is agreed between you and the buyer and paid to you.

## How much is my data worth?

It depends on the data: type, volume, age, quality and current buyer demand. The buyers I refer to publish ranges from around $50K to over $1M per company, but each one prices each company itself, and some sets won't be a fit. I'll tell you honestly after the first email, and I won't quote a number I can't stand behind.

## How long does it take?

I reply to every email within [TO FILL: e.g. 2 working days]. If there's a fit, the introduction usually follows within [TO FILL: e.g. 1–2 weeks]. The buyer's review, contract and data delivery take as long as the buyer and the size of the data set need.

## Do I have to sell my company?

No. You license a copy of the data. You keep the business, the systems and the original records.

## Can closed or liquidated companies take part?

Yes. For liquidators and insolvency practitioners, a company's records can be an extra asset to realise for creditors, at no cost to the estate. In a liquidation or bankruptcy, the liquidator or trustee usually has to approve the deal. The same rule applies: contract first, with de-identification agreed before anything is shared.

## Who are the buyers?

I take part in the referral programmes of a small number of AI-data buyers. I don't list them here because programmes open and close. Before any introduction I tell you exactly which buyer it is, and nothing happens without your OK.

## Is the résumé matcher private?

Yes. Your résumé is read and matched in your own browser. It isn't uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, and the page's security settings block it from sending data to other sites. Close the tab and it's gone.

## Does Dataceam hire for the AI-training roles?

No. The roles are posted by AI-training platforms. You apply on the platform's own site, and the platform screens, hires and pays you. If you're hired through one of my links, the platform may pay me a referral bonus. It costs you nothing.

## How do I start?

Email hello@dataceam.com with a line about your company and the records you hold. Everything happens by email until you decide to go ahead.
